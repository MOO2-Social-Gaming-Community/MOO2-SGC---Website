A new page, with old roots

The HTML and stylesheet for this edition were written afresh. The design follows a small, fixed-width fan homepage, not an in-game dashboard: a raster title, a space scene, ordinary links, serif text, simple tables, and a repeating star background.

The imagery is a mixture of existing MOO2 material supplied as references and a few new, small website decorations. The game images are not presented as original MOO2-SGC artwork.

Reference websites

  • MOO2MOD / ICE homepage — 23 February 2015 capture: centered title, space-station illustration, green headings, and small section images.
  • MicroProse MOO2 page — 21 December 1996 capture: small raster illustrations, a colony scene, and image-based presentation.
  • MOO2MOD: the continuing community site, resource links, and race portraits in the supplied saved-page bundle.

The references date from 1996, 2015, and the current site. This is an early-web-inspired design, not a claim that those pages existed in the early 1990s.

What this website does

This is a static fan homepage. It has no JavaScript, form submission, account system, visitor counter, advertising script, analytics tag, or third-party embed. Its images are hosted with the page, not hotlinked from the archives.

The layout is deliberately 760 pixels wide. It does not rearrange itself on narrow screens; horizontal scrolling may be needed. Browser zoom is not restricted. Text remains selectable, links can be reached with a keyboard, and images have alternative text.

The public community email is moo2-sgc@hotmail.com. The site also links to the MOO2-SGC GitHub Pages mirror, Game Blog, and Launcher & Installer repository. No chat invitation has been configured, and event dates and venues are not confirmed. Community wording remains a founding-stage draft.

These statements describe the website files only. Hosting providers and external sites linked from this page operate their own services.
